Research

Working paper2026

Largest-Shareholder Ownership and Post-Filing Corrections: Monitoring, Detection, and Disclosure

With Saiah Lee · Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology

Using a versioned OpenDART panel of 4,482 firm-years from 672 Korean listed nonfinancial firms (2018–2024), this project examines the relation between controlling-shareholder ownership and subsequent filing corrections. The analysis uses firm and year fixed effects, lagged ownership, within-between specifications, count models, placebo tests, and sensitivity analyses. The current evidence is interpreted as associational rather than causal.
