Working papers & selected research

Research

Working paper with Saiah Lee, UNIST 2026

Largest-Shareholder Ownership and Post-Filing Corrections: Monitoring, Detection, and Disclosure

Reconstructed versioned annual-report histories from Korea's OpenDART system, producing a verified panel of 4,482 firm-years from 672 listed nonfinancial firms over 2018–2024.

The study examines ownership concentration and post-filing corrections using fixed-effects, lagged-variable, within-between, placebo, and sensitivity analyses, with particular attention to monitoring, detection, disclosure incentives, and identification limits.

Solo-authored working paper · SSRN 7105059 2026

Designing Alliance Contracts under Uncertainty: Indexation, Renegotiation Risk, and the U.S.-ROK Special Measures Agreement

Studies the U.S.-ROK defense cost-sharing agreement as an incomplete contract, combining institutional analysis with contribution and inflation data to examine indexation, renegotiation risk, and contractual adaptation.
