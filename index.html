Working papers & selected research

Research

Working paper with Saiah Lee · UNIST 2026

Largest-Shareholder Ownership and Post-Filing Corrections: Monitoring, Detection, and Disclosure

Reconstructed versioned annual-report histories from Korea's OpenDART system, producing a verified panel of 4,482 firm-years from 672 listed nonfinancial firms over 2018–2024.

Examines ownership concentration and post-filing corrections using fixed-effects, lagged-variable, within-between, placebo, and sensitivity analyses, with particular attention to monitoring, detection, disclosure incentives, and identification limits.

Solo-authored working paper · SSRN 7105059 2026

Designing Alliance Contracts under Uncertainty: Indexation, Renegotiation Risk, and the U.S.-ROK Special Measures Agreement

Studies the U.S.-ROK defense cost-sharing agreement as an incomplete contract, combining institutional analysis with contribution and inflation data to examine indexation, renegotiation risk, and contractual adaptation.

AI Challengers Program · Second Prize 2025

FOMC Sentiment Index Using FinBERT

Constructed a time-series FOMC sentiment index by applying FinBERT to Federal Reserve statements and minutes. The project involved extensive data preprocessing, sentence-level sentiment scoring, an initial analysis of correlation with GDP, deployment to a real-time web dashboard, and subsequent lag analysis aimed at improving predictive usefulness.

Identification of Age-Related Genes in Skeletal Muscle Using Ensemble Machine Learning

Zenodo · Sep. 30, 2025

Uses GTEx transcriptomic data and XGBoost to identify genes associated with skeletal-muscle aging. The analysis highlights EDA2R, OPRD1, H2BC11, FAM171A1, GALNTL6, and DMRT2 as influential genes related to neuromuscular signaling, chromatin remodeling, and muscle-structure maintenance.
